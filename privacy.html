Privacy Policy

Final Cut: Missing Scene

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Final Cut: Missing Scene handles information. The core game can be played offline, does not require an account, and stores gameplay progress locally on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game uses local device storage to remember information such as your current editing stage, selected clips, watched footage, unlocked Editor's Notes, endings, tutorial state, and audio settings. This information remains on your device and can be removed by using Reset in the game or deleting the app.

Advertising and Device Information

The game may display advertising and may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you grant permission, advertising and fraud-prevention partners may process an advertising identifier and related device information. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not made available through that permission.

Advertising partners may independently process limited technical data such as IP address, device model, operating system, ad interactions, and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

The app includes advertising, analytics, and security components supplied by third parties, including Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, InMobi, and AppMetrica-related services. These services operate only when their configuration and network access are available. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Network Access

Network access may be used to request, load, measure, or protect advertising. The story, footage library, editing workflow, endings, settings, and local save system do not require an internet connection.

Data Retention and Control

Locally stored game progress remains until you reset progress or remove the app. Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to their own policies and legal obligations. You can limit tracking through iOS privacy settings and can deny the tracking permission request.

Children's Privacy

The game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game, legal requirements, or integrated services change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
